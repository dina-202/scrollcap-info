Scrollcap privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026
App: Scrollcap (com.scrollcap.app)
Developer: Dina_202

What Scrollcap does

Scrollcap counts short-video views and applies an optional daily cap. Counting, settings and history run on your device. The app has no internet permission, sign-in, advertising, analytics SDK, payment system or cloud sync.

Accessibility access

If you agree to the in-app disclosure and enable Android accessibility access, Scrollcap temporarily reads visible screen text, content descriptions, control identifiers, page changes and control positions in supported social apps. It uses those signals to identify short-video views, avoid duplicate counts, skip recognized ads and place the floating counter above a like or Favorite control. When your selected daily cap is reached, it can perform Android's Back action to leave that short-video feed.

Accessibility access is limited to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat and two TikTok package variants. Scrollcap uses the content of selected short-video feeds for these functions. Captions, creator names, messages and account names are not written to history, exported or transmitted. Temporary reel identities are held in memory and discarded with the session or process.

Information stored locally

The private app database stores social-app identifiers, viewing timestamps, reel counts and your preferences, including the daily cap and selected apps. Android protects the app's private storage. Scrollcap does not add its own database encryption. The developer cannot remotely read this database.

Background mode and notifications

Optional background monitoring uses an ongoing Android notification with Pause, Resume and Stop controls. Android may request notification permission. You control accessibility access, notifications and battery settings. Turning off monitoring stops counting; it does not erase history.

Sharing and export

Scrollcap does not send usage history or screen content to the developer or third parties. If you choose Export history, it writes a JSON file containing app identifiers and viewing timestamps to a location you select using Android's file picker. Export files are not encrypted by Scrollcap. Keep them private. Files saved to a synced folder or manually shared are handled by the storage or sharing provider you choose. Import reads only a file you select and merges valid history on your device.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and history remain until you clear Scrollcap's storage or uninstall it. Automatic Android backup and device transfer are disabled. Exported copies remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately. There is no online Scrollcap account or server copy to delete. Export history before uninstalling if you want to keep it.

Other services

Android, Google Play, social apps and any storage provider you choose operate under their own privacy policies. This policy describes Scrollcap's app behavior. Contacting support voluntarily shares the information you send with the support provider; do not send private usage history or sensitive screen content.

Changes and contact

Changes to these practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where needed, a new consent request.
For support or privacy inquiries, email dinalabs@proton.me. General support is also available at github.com/dina-202/scrollcap-info/issues. Do not include private information in a public issue.
